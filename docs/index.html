02Method

Evidence before narrative.

Every report keeps its source, timestamp, window and market definition attached to the research. A price move is not automatically a reason. Headlines remain context unless the evidence supports a stronger connection.

01 / Collect

Read the relevant market pairs and underlying reference data from their defined sources.

02 / Separate

Keep token performance, BNB-only volume, underlying performance and news in distinct evidence lanes.

03 / Explain

Describe what the company or ETF represents, which sector it serves and why the move may matter to a beginner.

04 / Compose

Build the report from approved SocratesOS visuals and add the exact figures deterministically after the artwork exists.
