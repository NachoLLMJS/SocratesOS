02Method

Evidence before narrative.

A price move is not automatically a reason. SocratesOS keeps each evidence lane separate until the relationship is strong enough to explain responsibly.

01

Collect

Read the defined market pairs and underlying reference data with their source, timestamp and window attached.

02

Separate

Keep token performance, BNB-only activity, underlying performance and news in distinct evidence lanes.

03

Explain

Describe what the company or ETF represents, which sector it serves and why the move may matter to a beginner.

04

Compose

Use approved SocratesOS visual language, add exact figures deterministically and keep uncertainty visible.

04X reports

From raw signal
to clear language.

A finished report tells the reader what moved, what the asset represents, which data window was used, what headline is relevant and what remains uncertain. It never presents correlation as proof.

// report shape
asset       tokenized stock + underlying
movement    price, volume, liquidity, activity
context     company, sector, chain, headlines
method      evidence separated from interpretation
output      concise X post + branded data image
boundary    uncertainty remains visible
Read the conclusion as a measured answer. The system can say that a source is missing, that a headline is only context, or that the evidence does not support a stronger causal claim.
05Builders

Built for context.

For builders, the important boundary is the separation between collection, interpretation, composition and publishing. Each layer can evolve without hiding where a figure or conclusion came from.

01

Named sources

Keep provider, timestamp, market definition and evidence window close to every output.

02

Manual confidence

When the evidence is incomplete, preserve the uncertainty instead of manufacturing a confident cause.

03

Deterministic output

Exact figures and approved visual language are added consistently after the research is complete.

04

Clear boundaries

Research, composition and publishing remain distinct so each stage can be audited.
